  The Yappologist — audio & video transcription
  by Dih (Darrel aka Marga's Wife)
════════════════════════════════════════════════

Thanks for trying The Yappologist! It turns any audio or video
file into a clean transcript with speaker labels and timestamps.

────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 1 — Install
────────────────────────────────────────
1. Double-click "The Yappologist.zip" to unzip it.
2. Drag "The Yappologist.app" into your Applications folder.

────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2 — Open it the first time (IMPORTANT)
────────────────────────────────────────
Because this is a free app made by hand (not from the App Store),
macOS will block it the first time with a message like:

   "The Yappologist can't be opened because Apple cannot
    check it for malicious software."

This is normal. To open it:

   1. Try to open the app (double-click it). You'll see the warning.
   2. Click the Apple menu  →  System Settings.
   3. Go to  Privacy & Security.
   4. Scroll down — you'll see a line about "The Yappologist
      was blocked." Click  "Open Anyway".
   5. Confirm, and enter your Mac password if asked.

You only have to do this ONCE. After that it opens normally.

────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 3 — Enter your key
────────────────────────────────────────
On first launch you'll see a welcome screen.
- Type your name.
- Type the resource name that was sent to you.
- Paste the API key that was sent to you separately.
- Pick a theme (Light, Dark, or Cute 🍎).
- Click "Get started".

You need BOTH the resource name and the key — they were sent
to you together. The key is stored safely in your Mac's Keychain.
If macOS asks to allow access to the keychain, click "Always
Allow" and enter your Mac password.

────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 4 — Transcribe
────────────────────────────────────────
- Drag one file onto the drop zone — or drag a whole batch, or
  click "Choose files" and select several at once.
- Files line up in a queue and transcribe one after another.
  Each one shows its own progress bar, and you can cancel,
  retry, or remove any of them while the rest keep going.
- Drop more files onto the queue at any time to add them.
- You'll get a notification when a file finishes if you've
  switched to something else.
- When a file finishes, click "View" to read its transcript.
  (Drop just one file and it opens automatically.)

Supported: mp3, wav, mp4, mov, flac, aac, ogg, opus, webm, m4a
(and large/odd files are auto-converted for you).

────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 5 — Fix and listen
────────────────────────────────────────
No transcription is perfect, especially with Taglish. So:

- Click any line to edit it. Changes save automatically.
- Click a timestamp to hear that exact moment.
- Change playback speed (0.5× to 2×) while you check.
- Turn on "Follow" and the transcript scrolls with the audio.
- Got the wrong speaker? Click the "Speaker 1" badge and
  reassign that block.
- Search with ⌘F. Type a replacement and hit "Replace all"
  to fix the same mistake everywhere at once.

Handy keys:  ⌘F search  ·  ⌘P play/pause  ·  ⌘← ⌘→ jump between
speakers.

────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 6 — Export
────────────────────────────────────────
Copy the transcript, or export it as Word, PDF, or .txt.
Exports are named after the original recording.

Everything you transcribe is saved. Click "History" to reopen
old transcripts, or export them all to a folder at once.

────────────────────────────────────────
Settings (gear icon, or Command + comma)
────────────────────────────────────────
- Change your key, name or theme.
- Custom vocabulary: list names and jargon, one per line, and
  it will listen out for them. This is the best way to fix
  words it keeps getting wrong.
- Auto-corrections: write rules like  wrong => right  and they
  are applied to every new transcript automatically.
- Remove fillers (um, uh) if you want a tidier transcript.
- Choose whether exports include timestamps and speaker labels.

Enjoy! 💜
